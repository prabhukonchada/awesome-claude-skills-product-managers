# Social Signal Research

Turn public social posts into product discovery evidence with source links,
caveats, and follow-up decisions.

## Use Cases

- Launch response review
- Competitor mention analysis
- Customer language mining
- Community pain-signal synthesis
- Evidence gathering before a PRD, experiment, or prioritization decision

## Trigger Phrases

- "Research social feedback for this feature"
- "Analyze X/Twitter reactions to this launch"
- "Find public objections around this competitor"
- "Summarize market pain signals from social posts"
- "Use Xquik to collect X/Twitter evidence"

## What It Produces

- A research question
- A bounded collection plan
- Evidence themes with source links
- Caveats for bias, missing coverage, and stale data
- A recommended next product validation step
